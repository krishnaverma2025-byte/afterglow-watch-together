Connect your browser

  1. Chrome is selected. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. Afterglow has already been loaded in this Chrome profile.
  2. Select Documents/ChatGPT/REA/watch-together/extension. Open Afterglow from the Chrome toolbar.
  3. Open the Afterglow extension popup. Copy its full extension origin and pair it below.
  4. Open a movie or episode on the streaming site and start playback. Open the extension again to create a party.
  5. Send its invitation link to your friends. Each person opens the same title in their own account, then joins using the extension.

Folder: Documents/ChatGPT/REA/watch-together/extension
Pairing lasts until the local server restarts. No account credentials are requested.

What connects?

The extension connects your open video player to the party server. Video, account passwords, cookies, and DRM keys stay with the streaming service. Only title identity, playback position, controls, and chat are synchronized.

Safari on your Mac

Safari source preparation and origin pairing are included. Building the containing macOS app requires full Xcode, which is missing here. No installable Safari app or live compatibility result is available yet.

Before inviting remote friends

The development extension connects to localhost:3000. That address is your Mac only. Internet parties require a shared HTTPS backend and an updated extension backend/host-permission configuration.

Known limits

Provider pages and private player APIs can change. Ads, live channels, cross-country title IDs, iframe-only players, and episode changes are not supported end-to-end. Recognized ads suspend sync; they are never skipped. This version does not automatically switch episodes. Use the same on-demand title and compatible account tiers.
